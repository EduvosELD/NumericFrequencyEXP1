Numeric Frequency Distribution Exercise 1

Learn how to create a Numeric Frequency Distribution by using the data provided

The Scenario: The Midterm Exam Meltdown

You are Teaching Assistant for a large university statistics class of 50 students.

The midterm exam was incredibly tough, and the professor just posted a raw, unorganized list of scores on the notice board.

Looking at a random list of 50 numbers between 40 and 100 makes it impossible to see the bigger picture. Are most people failing? Did anyone ace it? Where does your own score fit in?

To find out, your job is to, construct a grouped numeric frequency distribution table.
